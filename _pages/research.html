My research sits at the intersection of physics-guided deep learning, generative modeling, and computational inverse problems, with a focus on medical image reconstruction under challenging acquisition conditions — sparse measurements, limited angular coverage, and noisy data. I develop AI systems that are not only data-driven but also grounded in the physics of imaging, aiming for models that generalize reliably to real clinical settings.

--- ## Research Directions --- ### 🔬 Sparse-View CT Reconstruction

Reducing X-ray dose in CT scanning requires reconstructing diagnostic-quality images from far fewer projections than standard protocols. My work in this direction focuses on developing physics-constrained generative adversarial networks that enforce sinogram-domain data consistency while learning powerful image-domain priors. Key contributions include dual-stage architectures with adaptive gating mechanisms and physics-informed attention modules that guide reconstruction toward anatomically faithful outputs.

**Representative works:** - *PIDA-GAN: Physics-Informed Dual-Stage Attention GAN with Data Consistency for Sparse-View CT Reconstruction* — Under Review, 2026. - *PCDS-GAN: Physics-Constrained Dual-Stage Generative Model with Adaptive Gating for Ultra Sparse-View CT Reconstruction* — IEEE ISBI 2026 **(Oral)**. - *Novel Dual-Stage GAN for High-Fidelity CT Image Reconstruction from Sparse-View CT* — JAMIT 2025. --- ### 🔬 Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction

Limited-angle CT arises in interventional imaging, security screening, and radiation therapy planning, where the acquisition arc is severely restricted. Missing angular data introduces severe streaking artifacts and structural ambiguities. I address this through frequency-domain learning and locality-guided attention mechanisms that selectively recover high-frequency structures lost due to the incomplete angular range, combined with GAN-based frameworks trained on physics-consistent objectives.

**Representative works:** - *FLoAT-GAN: Frequency- and Locality-Guided Attention for Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction* — AAAI-26 W3PHIAI Workshop **(Long Presentation)**. - *Multi-Frequency Domains with Modified U-Net for Limited-Angle CT* — IEEE ISBI 2026 **(Poster, Best Presentation Award 🏆)**. - *Attention-Guided Frequency and Locality Learning GAN for LACT* — IWAIT/IFMIA 2026. - *An Attention-Based Generative Adversarial Network for Limited-Angle CT Reconstruction* — ICCAE 2025 **(Best Presentation Award 🏆)**. --- ### 🔬 Physics-Guided Deep Learning for Inverse Problems

A recurring theme across my work is the integration of forward imaging models into deep learning pipelines. Rather than treating reconstruction as a black-box mapping, I embed Radon transform-based consistency constraints, frequency-domain priors, and sinogram-domain supervision directly into network training. This physics-AI co-design philosophy improves robustness under distribution shift and enables meaningful generalization beyond the training data.

**Representative works:** - *PIDA-GAN* — Under Review, 2026. - *PCDS-GAN* — IEEE ISBI 2026. - *Compressed Sensing-Based Image Reconstruction for Discrete Tomography with Sparse View and Limited Angle Geometries* — PLOS ONE, 2025. --- ### 🔬 Mathematical Methods & Level-Set Tomography

My Ph.D. research introduced parametric level-set methods for geometric and binary tomographic reconstruction, where the object to be recovered is represented implicitly as a level set of a smooth parametric function. This framework provides a principled way to incorporate shape priors and handle extreme data sparsity, and forms the mathematical foundation for my broader interest in model-based reconstruction.
