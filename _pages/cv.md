## Summary

I am a JSPS Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of Tsukuba, Japan, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and medical imaging. My research focuses on computational imaging, inverse problems, and deep learning, with an emphasis on developing robust and interpretable AI systems for medical image reconstruction and analysis.

I received my Ph.D. in Engineering from the University of Tsukuba, where I worked on mathematical modeling and reconstruction methods for sparse-view and limited-angle computed tomography (CT). My work integrates physics-guided deep learning, generative modeling, and domain knowledge to address challenges in limited or imperfect data settings.

My research has been supported by competitive funding, including the JSPS Research Fellowship and KAKENHI grants. I have published in leading journals and conferences such as PLOS ONE, ISBI, and ICCAE, and received multiple awards for research presentations.
